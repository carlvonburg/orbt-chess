---
name: store-screenshots
description: Make the App Store, Google Play and Microsoft Store screenshots and store graphics of ORBT Chess from the sample repertoires.
---
Every set looks the same: a one-line headline over a glow in the feature's color (orange for
gaps, blue for building, green for learning), the real screen in a device frame, and one
overlay that lifts a single element out of it, a different kind on each shot. Overlays are
redrawn, but only with what the screen under them shows. The first three shots carry the pitch
(gaps, choosing a move, studying). Outputs go to `<store>/screenshots/` and raw captures to
`raw/`; neither is committed. Upload in file order.

## The data (every store)
1. `Design/AppStore/samples/*.pgn`: Italian Game as White, Najdorf and Queen's Gambit Declined as
   Black, generated by `Design/AppStore/tools` (`cargo run --release --bin gen`) from the Blitz
   1600 coverage table; `tools/build_pgn.py` takes out two branches each so there are gaps.
2. `tools/src/bin/sim.rs` studies them day by day through the core's `Drill` (about 10 weeks, one
   wrong answer in eleven), so counts, streak and graphs are the scheduler's own. Date it so the
   last study day is yesterday.
3. `tools/storeshots.py <abs raw dir> <abs out dir> <set> [shot …]` renders each shot from
   `tools/shots.json` (headline, color, overlays) at the exact size (Pillow; headless Chrome or
   Edge). When the UI changes, recapture `raw/` and check every overlay still matches its screen.

## App Store (sets `iphone`, `ipad`, `mac`)
- iPhone 6.3": iPhone 17 Pro Max captures, 1206 × 2622. iPad 13": iPad Pro 13-inch (M5),
  2752 × 2064 landscape. Mac: window captures, 2880 × 1800.
- Simulator: files in the app's Documents (iCloud off), `xcrun simctl status_bar <id> override
  --time 9:41 …`, locale en_US, light appearance.
- Mac: a separate build (`PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER=org.orbtchess.orbt.screens`, ad-hoc signed,
  sandbox without iCloud, so it never touches a real install; deny its Keychain request),
  launched with `--args -AppleLocale en_US -AppleLanguages '(en-US)'`, window 1400 × 900,
  captured with ⌘⇧4 Space ⌥-click, trimmed to the window.

## Google Play (sets `android-phone`, `android-tablet`)
- Phone 1080 × 1920, tablet 2560 × 1440; Play wants 9:16 or 16:9, 2–8 each. No desktop shots.
  The Android app's theme colors, set in Roboto.
- Emulators Pixel 9 Pro XL and Pixel Tablet (landscape), cold-booted so the clock is the Mac's,
  the debug APK, status bar in demo mode (9:41, full battery and Wi-Fi, no notifications). Copy the
  data into the app's `files/Repertoires` with `run-as`; capture with `adb exec-out screencap -p`.
- Graphics: `rsvg-convert -w 512 -h 512 Design/AppIcon-light.svg -o Design/PlayStore/icon-512.png`
  and `rsvg-convert -w 1024 -h 500 Design/PlayStore/feature-graphic.svg -o Design/PlayStore/feature-graphic.png`.

## Microsoft Store (set `windows`, on Windows)
- Desktop 3840 × 2160, light mode, Segoe UI, the window as Windows draws it.
- The core's DLL (`cargo build --release -p orbt-ffi --target x86_64-pc-windows-gnu` in `core/`,
  copied to `android/core/build/host/`), then `./gradlew :app:createDistributable`.
- Data in `<folder>/ORBT Chess/`, Blitz 1600's coverage table and `manifest.json` in
  `<folder>/Support/Coverage/`. Start `app/build/compose/binaries/main/app/ORBT Chess/ORBT Chess.exe`
  with `JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS="-Dorbt.dataDir=<folder> -Dsun.java2d.uiScale=1.5 -Duser.language=en -Duser.country=US"`
  (kept apart from a real install; 1.5× is sharp enough for 4K).
- `Design/MicrosoftStore/capture.ps1 <file> -Width 2100 -Height 1300` sizes and captures the
  window, then `capture.ps1 <file>` per screen: Today, the Najdorf overview, Browse/Edit on 5…a6,
  Bc4 there, Analysis once the cloud eval shows depth 46, and Queen's Gambit Declined › Study Now
  with 3…c5 played (the line plays on within a second: take a burst).
- Logos: `art/poster-art.png` (1440 × 2160) and `art/box-art.png` (2160 × 2160), exported at twice
  the SVG size: `rsvg-convert -z 2 art/poster-art.svg -o art/poster-art.png`.
