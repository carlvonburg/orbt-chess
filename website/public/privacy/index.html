Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

In short: ORBT Chess collects no data. There are no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no ORBT account. The developer never receives your repertoires, your training history or anything else about you.

Your files

Your repertoires, their settings and your review history are files on your device. They sync between your devices only through a service you choose in Settings: iCloud Drive (Apple devices), Dropbox or Google Drive. ORBT has no server of its own that sees them.

Dropbox and Google Drive (optional)

If you choose one, the app keeps your repertoire files in an ORBT Chess folder in your account and can see only that folder. The files go straight from your device to your account, never through the developer, under the Dropbox or Google privacy policy. The sign-in is stored encrypted on your device, and your email address is only shown there. Signing out removes the sign-in; your files stay in your account.

ORBT Chess's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app sends, and to whom

Like any internet request, each of these reveals your IP address to the server it goes to.

  • Lichess (lichess.org, explorer.lichess.org): chess positions, to fetch opening statistics from the opening explorer and cloud evaluations for the analysis board. Only positions are sent, never your repertoire as a whole. Lichess's handling of these requests is covered by the Lichess privacy policy.
  • Lichess sign-in (optional): if you sign in, the app receives an access token from Lichess, keeps it in your device's Keychain and sends it only to Lichess with the requests above. Signing out revokes it at Lichess and deletes it from your device.
  • Analyse on Lichess: when you choose it, the current line opens on lichess.org in your browser.
  • Coverage tables: offline opening statistics are downloaded from a Cloudflare R2 storage bucket. The download request carries nothing about you or your repertoire.
  • Engine network: if you download Stockfish's full network in Settings, it comes from tests.stockfishchess.org.

Stored on your device

Opening statistics already fetched are cached on your device so each position is fetched once. App preferences are kept in the app's own settings. Neither leaves your device.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send anonymous crash reports, which the developer can see in Xcode. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Open source

You don't have to take our word for any of this: the app's full source code is public.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: hello@orbtchess.org.
