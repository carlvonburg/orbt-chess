Join the beta

In public beta until the launch on 1 December: your feedback shapes the 1.0.

ORBT Chess is free on every platform: no subscription, no ads, no account.

Android

Android 10 or later, phones and tablets

With the Google account you use on your phone or tablet:

  1. Join the testers group.
  2. Become a tester, then install ORBT Chess from Google Play.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the link on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. One beta for all three, with your repertoires in iCloud Drive.

Join on TestFlight

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, x64

Download the installer (.msi)

The installer doesn't update itself: download the new one when there is a release. Windows asks once before running it, since it isn't signed: choose More info › Run anyway. The Microsoft Store version comes on 1 December.

Linux

x64, Debian, Ubuntu and other distributions that install .deb packages

Download the package (.deb)

Install it from the folder you downloaded it to:

sudo apt install ./orbt-chess_amd64.deb

It doesn't update itself: download the new package when there is a release.

Send feedback

Tell us what works, what breaks and what's missing:

  • On iPhone, iPad and Mac: take a screenshot in the app and share it as beta feedback, or use Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
  • Everywhere: report a bug or suggest an idea on GitHub, or write to us from the contact page.

Syncing your repertoires

Your repertoires and training progress sync between all your devices: choose Dropbox or Google Drive in Settings › Sync on each, or iCloud Drive if all of them are Apple devices. They're plain PGN files, and on Windows and Linux you'll also find them in the ORBT Chess folder in your Documents folder, where you can drop in PGNs from other apps.

Other downloads

Every release, its checksums (SHA-256) and its source code are on GitHub Releases; to hear about new ones, watch the repository's releases. You can also build it yourself.
